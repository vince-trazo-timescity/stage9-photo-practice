Email 4 • Edit

Correction clinic: clarity first

Editor’s rule

Do not change a sentence only because it “sounds strange.” Identify the problem: tense, passive form, agreement, word order or unclear reference.

Email 5 • Develop

Grow an opinion into persuasive reasoning

Too thin

In my opinion, a quiet hour is good.

Developed

Stance: In my opinion, a quiet hour could improve students’ wellbeing.

Reason: One reason for this is that constant notifications prevent students from mentally resting.

Example: For example, a pupil who answers group messages throughout lunch may return to class feeling distracted rather than refreshed.

Balance: However, students should be allowed to use their phones in emergencies.
